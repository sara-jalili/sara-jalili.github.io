About me

I completed my B.Sc. at Amirkabir University of Technology and my M.Sc. in Computer Science at the University of Alberta under the supervision of James R. Wright . My master’s research focused on using optimization and simulation to study spectrum auctions. I now work as a Machine Learning Researcher at Algonet, where I investigate recent methods in multimodal AI and computer vision, evaluate models on established benchmarks, and develop algorithms to improve their performance on visual reasoning tasks.
